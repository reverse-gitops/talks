###### Hard part 4 · The objection

# "So users should open pull requests?"

<v-clicks>

- Your **users** will never open a pull request
- Infrastructure-as-code is an extra layer **on top of** the API you already built
- You lose the quick experiment: change it, look, change it back
- The people who change settings: admins, support staff, scripts, **AI agents**

</v-clicks>

<p v-click><strong>History, review and rollback are fair things to ask for.</strong> Why does it take an extra layer to get them?</p>
