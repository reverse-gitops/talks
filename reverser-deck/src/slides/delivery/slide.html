REVERSER · DELIVERY
Ship settings like images.
Settings ship as an OCI artifact. Flux picks up new versions, and the Deployment mounts them as a read-only image volume.
01 Bob’s revert lands in Git and is published as a new OCI artifact: orderer-config:43.
02 The registry holds the app image and every version of its settings, side by side.
03 Flux image automation sees the new tag, bumps the Deployment in Git, and applies it. No pipeline to babysit.
04 Settings arrive as a read-only image volume. No settings resources live in the infra cluster.
05 And the app just reads YAML files. A typed library, generated from the CRDs, makes that a few lines of code.
